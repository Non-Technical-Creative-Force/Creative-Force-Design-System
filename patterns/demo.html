01 — Colour

Semantic roles first

Reach for a role, not a hue. The stone ramp is the warm neutral spine; blue is the one accent, roughly three uses per view.

02 — Typography

Inter, and one ladder

Size, weight, line-height and tracking travel together, so 44px cannot be applied at the wrong weight. Headings are 600, never 700.

03 — Cards on a warm band

Surfaces are flat fills

A card on a warm band is surface-raised. No grain, no gradient, no overlay — the texture belongs inside shapes, never behind them.

Style guides

04 — Data is quiet

One hero number, not a strip

110px is reserved for a single moment per page. A four-up band of huge white numerals on black is the cliché this brand is defined against.

05 — Illustration

The cube states mean something

Neutral is before. Solid blue is activated. Ghost is potential. Never scatter them as decoration.

06 — Logo wall

The gaps are the rules

1px grid gaps expose the edge colour underneath. Logos cap at 68% wide and 38px tall so unlike marks read at one optical weight.

07 — Icons

Streamline Core Line, one style

Thin even stroke, currentColor, 20–24px. Never Font Awesome, never Heroicons, never emoji.

08 — Layout

Opt out to go wide

Everything is measure-capped by default. The data-w attribute is how a block escapes.

This paragraph sits in the content track — capped at the reading measure, clamp(45ch, 60vw, 68ch), tuned to Inter. It is the default, so it needs no attribute.

data-w="popout" — one deliberate step wider.
data-w="feature" — a bigger step, for media.
data-w="full" — gutter to gutter.
